# Outreach kit: getting your first deals

**Goal:** contact 25 real estate agents or brokers a day and offer them a **free sample on WhatsApp**. Then talk to them directly in the same chat and close the sale.

**Why WhatsApp:** once the sample goes out on WhatsApp, you are already in a chat with the buyer. You can ask straight away whether they want the full file, answer questions, and send the payment link, all in one place.

## 1. How a sample request reaches you

There are two ways:

1. **Someone fills in the form on the website.** They enter their WhatsApp number, and you get an email.
   - The email has an **"Open the chat with them"** link. Tap it on your phone and WhatsApp opens with their number and the first message already typed.
   - Attach the sample file, send it, and ask: *"Would you like the full file?"*
2. **Someone replies to your outreach.** They either send their WhatsApp number or tap your WhatsApp link above. Either way, they're already in your WhatsApp.

**Send the sample within 1 hour if you can.** The faster you reply, the more likely they are to buy.

## 2. Where to find people

### Dubai brokers (Owner Database $300, Dubai Buyer Leads $1,200)
- **Property Finder and Bayut:** open any listing and you'll see the agent's name, agency and WhatsApp number. Agents with lots of listings are active, so they're the best targets.
- **LinkedIn:** search for "real estate broker Dubai", "property consultant Dubai" or "RERA broker".
- **Dubai REST app / DLD broker directory:** an official list of registered brokers.
- **Agency owners and team leaders** are the best targets, because one sale can mean the whole team uses the data.

### Miami and California agents (buyer leads, $1,500 each)
- **LinkedIn:** search for "Realtor Miami", "Brickell real estate agent", "Los Angeles realtor" or "Orange County real estate agent".
- **Zillow and Realtor.com agent directories**, filtered by city.
- **Brokerage websites** (eXp, Compass, Keller Williams office pages).

### UK agents (UK Buyer Leads, $750)
- **LinkedIn:** "estate agent London", "property consultant Manchester".
- **Rightmove** branch pages.

## 3. Rules so you don't get blocked or fined

- **Dubai:** you can WhatsApp agents directly. Message only between 9am and 6pm, and if someone says "stop", never message them again.
- **USA:** **never send the first message to a US mobile number on WhatsApp or SMS.** That can break the TCPA, and the fines are large. Make first contact on **LinkedIn or email** and ask for their WhatsApp number. Once **they** send you their number or message you first, WhatsApp is fine.
- **UK:** make first contact by LinkedIn or a work email (info@, office@), then move to WhatsApp once they agree.
- **Everywhere:**
  - Send messages by hand, one at a time.
  - No more than 25–30 a day on LinkedIn, or LinkedIn will restrict the account.
  - Every email needs an opt-out line.

## 5. On WhatsApp: from sample to sale

**When you send the sample:**
> Hi [name], here's your free sample of the [file name]. They're real records from this month's file, in the same Excel columns as the full dataset. Call a few and see how they respond. Any questions, just ask here.

**Same day, a few hours later:**
> Did the file open OK? Is [area] the main area you work, or should I check other areas for you too?

**After 2 days:**
> Hi [name], did you get a chance to call any of the sample contacts? How did they respond?

**When they're interested:**
> The full [file name] is $[price], a one-time payment with no subscription. It's delivered in 10 minutes, and you can pay by card or USDT. There's also a 7-day refund policy. Shall I send you the payment link?

**After 5 days, if they've gone quiet:**
> Hi [name], just checking in. If now isn't the right time, no problem. The sample's yours to keep, and I'm here if you want the full file later.

**Tip:** a WhatsApp **voice note** (30 seconds, friendly, using their name) often gets more replies than text.

**"Can I get a discount?"**
Decide on a rule first and stick to it. For example, 10% off if they buy two files together. Don't give a discount to everyone who asks.
